## English

For the Yibin campus of Sichuan University of Science & Engineering, on 64-bit Windows 10/11. Supports campus Ethernet and `SUSE_Hostel` / `SUSE` Wi-Fi.

### Changes

- Automatically loads current campus services and adds 刷新服务列表 (Refresh service list).
- Select and save again if the school renames your service; the selection is retained after restarting.
- Retains wired priority, background automatic login and system notifications.

### Download and upgrade

**[Download Windows v1.1.3](https://github.com/tsiskjsjs532-hash/suse-campus-login/releases/download/v1.1.3/SUSE-CampusLogin-Windows-v1.1.3.zip)**

Extract the complete ZIP and open `CampusLogin.exe`. Enter your own account and password, select a service, then click 保存并测试 (Save and test). Windows protects the password stored on your PC.

Exit the old app from the tray before opening the new version. Existing settings are retained. If you use startup, save once in the new version to update its startup location.
